## 1. Overview & Human Subject / Expert Participant Protections

This checklist establishes the formal informed consent, blinding commitments, and data-handling standards required of all human experts participating in the PahaarPulse evaluation.

---

## Part A: Voluntary Participation & Informed Consent

- [ ] **1. Voluntary Participation:** Reviewer confirms that their participation is completely voluntary and that they may withdraw at any time prior to submission locking without penalty.
- [ ] **2. Project Scope Understanding:** Reviewer understands that annotations will serve as ground-truth evaluation references for open-science mountain hazard screening research.
- [ ] **3. Institutional / IRB Compliance:** If affiliated with an academic or governmental research institution, reviewer has confirmed whether participation requires local institutional ethics clearance or departmental notification.
- [ ] **4. Compensation / Acknowledgment Agreed:** Terms regarding honorarium, academic co-authorship, or formal acknowledgment have been agreed upon in writing with the Project Owner.

---

## Part B: Confidentiality, Blinding & Search Prohibition

- [ ] **5. Double-Blinding Commitment:** Reviewer agrees not to seek information regarding companion reviewers, model zero-shot predictions, or algorithmic prompt transcripts.
- [ ] **6. Strict Reviewer Independence:** Reviewer commits to completing all annotations in isolation without conferring with any other engineering or geological colleague.
- [ ] **7. Reverse-Image Search Ban:** Reviewer commits under honor not to execute reverse image lookups (Google Lens, TinEye, Bing Visual Search) or search file names online.
- [ ] **8. Native Resolution Viewing:** Reviewer agrees to evaluate all images at 100% native uncompressed scale on a calibrated $\ge 1080\text{p}$ monitor.

---

## Part C: Data Security & Attribution Protection

- [ ] **9. Secure Local File Custody:** Reviewer agrees to store evaluation packages on password-protected personal or institutional hardware and not upload unreleased packages to cloud AI tools.
- [ ] **10. Attribution Protection:** Reviewer acknowledges that the image collection comprises openly licensed photographs (Creative Commons, Public Domain) and agrees not to redistribute images without preserving original attribution registers.
- [ ] **11. Conflict of Interest Notification:** Reviewer agrees to disclose any prior personal or commercial involvement with any site depicted in the evaluation imagery.
