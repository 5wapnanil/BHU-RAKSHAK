## 1. Overview & Operational Purpose

This recruitment pack contains all necessary operational guides, interview questionnaires, invitation templates, qualification rubrics, capacity planning worksheets, and candidate trackers for recruiting qualified human geotechnical experts for the PahaarPulse visual landslide screening evaluation.

The goal is to transition PahaarPulse safely and transparently from technical workflow verification (Phase 2L) to **authoritative human-reviewed ground truth** by securing:
- Two independent qualified human annotators (Reviewer 1 and Reviewer 2).
- One senior geotechnical adjudicator for consensus dispute resolution.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Data Privacy & Minimal Collection Invariant:**  
> To protect personal privacy and comply with research ethics standards, **no real names, phone numbers, home addresses, government ID copies, or confidential employment contracts may be committed to this repository**.
> Real candidate contact information must be maintained exclusively by the Project Owner in an offline administrative registry. Only pseudonymous identifiers (`CAND_01`, `REV_A1`, `REV_B2`) may appear in version-controlled trackers.
